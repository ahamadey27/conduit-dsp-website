Getting variations out of a finite pool of samples can get you in the dumps. Whether you're manually applying volume and pitch adjustments in your DAW or trying to figure out how to get the round robin feature on your sampler to work, these simple, repetitive tasks can really harsh your workflow. Enter, Robin Control Lite. A single voice sampler dedicated to getting the most seamless variation and randomization out of your samples.

Robin Control Lite offers controlled parameter randomization for pitch, volume, pan, tone and sample start/end points from a pool of up to 20 samples, played back in series or shuffled. Plus, you can easily reorder, delete, replace and audition your samples. Not sure where to start? The Algorithm Knob generates immediate randomization — one knob that makes your samples a little more rando with every click.

A simple plugin meant to solve, at times, a time-consuming task — free of charge.

Under the hood.

  • Dead Simple UX/UI: Every MIDI key triggers the current sample pool at its natural pitch, with controls designed for quick, intuitive adjustments.
  • Sample Playback Feedback: Visual feedback in the sample pool shows which sample is playing.
  • Adjustable Interface Size: Use the interface scaling button to choose a plugin window size that fits your screen.
  • Pro Tools Support: AAX is included for Pro Tools on macOS and Windows, alongside VST3 on both platforms and AU on macOS.
  • True Monophonic Round Robin Playback: Samples cycle intelligently in series or random order so no two hits ever sound identical — essential for natural-sounding SFX and percussion
  • Asymmetric Randomization Engine: Every knob has independent upper and lower randomization bounds, letting you dial in controlled chaos — slightly off-pitch hits, unpredictable dynamics, and subtle EQ drift all at once
  • Algorithm Knob Selector: Choose from 17 different algorithm presets to start getting immediate variation from your sample pool. Randomization intensity starts very subtle and moves up to extreme
  • Global Pitch Control with Per-Hit Randomization: Semitone and Fine Tune knobs with randomizable ranges give you both macro transposition and micro-pitch variation on every single trigger
  • 2-Band Tone EQ with Randomizable Frequency: High and low shelf eq to shape your tone instantly
  • Intuitive Sample Manager: Reorder, audition, replace, and delete samples directly in the plugin panel without touching your DAW browser
  • Sample Start/End Trimming: Percentage-based start and end points with built-in micro-fades — trim room noise and tails directly inside the plugin, no external editor needed
  • Lightweight & Zero-Latency: Monophonic voice architecture with minimal CPU overhead — load dozens of instances in a large game audio session without breaking a sweat.
  • Built for Game Audio & Post Production: Designed, but not limited to, one-shot SFX workflows — footsteps, impacts, foley, UI sounds — not repurposed from a general-purpose sampler.
