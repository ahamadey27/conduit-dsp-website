Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026 · This policy applies to conduitdsp.com and all Conduit DSP products.

Conduit DSP LLC ("Conduit DSP," "we," "our," or "us") is a small, independent audio software company based in New York. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we use it. We keep things simple because we are a small operation and we only collect what we actually need.

If you have questions about anything here, email us at hello@conduitdsp.com.

1. Information We Collect

Email address. When you download a free plugin, join a waitlist, subscribe to our newsletter, or purchase a product, we receive the email address you provide. We use it to deliver downloads and service messages. If you opt in to marketing, we also send occasional news about future Conduit DSP products, updates, and discounts. A free license does not require newsletter enrollment. We will not bombard you, and every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link.

Accounts and orders. Moonbase provides our customer accounts, checkout, licenses, and downloads. We receive account and order details such as your name, email, products, license status, and relevant billing information. Payment details are handled by Moonbase and its payment providers; we do not receive your full card number. Earlier orders and receipts may remain with Lemon Squeezy.

Plugin activation. Robin Control Lite 2.0.0 uses Moonbase for activation, validation, and deactivation. This processes license/account details, a device identifier and label, IP address, and software-version information. The plugin stores a signed license locally, including the license holder’s name and email. Optional plugin analytics are disabled, and the plugin does not upload your samples, presets, or DAW projects. Permanent offline activation exchanges request and license files using another connected device. Earlier DRM-free versions retain their original offline behavior.

Optional website analytics. If you choose “Allow analytics” in our site banner, Google Analytics may collect information such as pages visited, approximate location, referral source, browser/device type, and time on site. We use it to understand how people find us and which content is useful. Analytics does not load if you choose “No thanks.”

Support communications. If you email us for support, we keep that email thread so we can follow up and improve our products. We do not share those conversations with anyone.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To send you your plugin download link after you sign up
  • To send the marketing and product-update emails you subscribe to (you can unsubscribe at any time)
  • To manage accounts, fulfill orders, provide downloads, and activate and validate licenses through Moonbase
  • To respond to support requests
  • To understand how our website is being used so we can improve it

We do not sell your email address or any personal information to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising targeting on other platforms.

3. Third-Party Services We Use

We use a small number of third-party tools to run our business. Each has its own privacy policy that governs how they handle data:

  • MailerLite — Email marketing and list management. Your email address is stored in MailerLite when you subscribe. MailerLite Privacy Policy
  • Moonbase — Customer accounts, checkout, license delivery, downloads, activation, and communication preferences. Our Moonbase integration synchronizes customer details, subscription status, and product-related groups to MailerLite. Moonbase Privacy Policy
  • Lemon Squeezy — Earlier checkout, payment, and download records and receipt access. Lemon Squeezy Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics — Optional website analytics, loaded only after you allow it. Google Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Pages — Website hosting. GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Namecheap — Domain registration and DNS.
  • Cloudflare — Secure infrastructure used for limited store and email-list integrations. Cloudflare Privacy Policy

We are selective about the tools we use. We do not currently use Meta Pixel, Google Ads tracking, or behavioral advertising scripts on this website.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

The site stores your analytics choice locally. Moonbase uses browser storage and cookies for storefront functions such as cart state and account sessions, and receives network information when the embedded storefront loads. These functions are separate from optional website analytics. Old cart data may remain in your browser from the previous store; it is not transferred into Moonbase. If you allow analytics, Google Analytics may set analytics cookies. We do not use third-party advertising cookies, and shopping, downloads, and the rest of the site continue to work if you reject analytics.

5. Data Retention

We keep your email address on our active list for as long as you remain subscribed. If you unsubscribe, we stop marketing to that address, while retaining only the records needed to honor your request and meet legal or operational obligations. Purchase records are retained as required for business, tax, fraud-prevention, and support purposes.

6. Your Rights

You can unsubscribe from our email list at any time using the link at the bottom of any email we send. You can also email us to request that we delete your data, correct inaccurate information, or find out exactly what information we hold about you. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are located in the European Union or United Kingdom, you have additional rights under GDPR, including the right to data portability and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Email us and we will work with you directly.

7. Children

Our products and website are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top and, if the changes are significant, notify our email list. Continued use of our site or products after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
